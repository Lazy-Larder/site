About

Made by a household, for households

Lazy Larder was created, and is looked after, by a husband and wife duo, Ross and Amanda.

Why we built it

We built Lazy Larder to solve our own meal planning and shopping list problems.

The hope is that it makes planning your weekly meals and building your shopping list so much easier that your larder becomes lazy – but you won't.

What we believe

  • ✓No AI. There's a time and a place for it, and we don't think it's in Lazy Larder.
  • ✓Privacy first. No email address or password – everything goes through your own iCloud.
  • ✓Recipe websites are great. We're not trying to replace them – just to make your favourites easier to keep and cook from.
